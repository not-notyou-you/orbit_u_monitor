URUTAN DI LAPTOP BARU  (rinciannya di DOCS/SETUP_LAPTOP_BARU.md §5-§6)
  1. psql -U postgres -f roles.sql
  2. createdb -U postgres themonitor
  3. psql -U postgres -d themonitor -c "CREATE EXTENSION postgis; CREATE EXTENSION pgcrypto;"
  4. pg_restore -U postgres -d themonitor --no-owner --role=postgres -j 4 themonitor.dump
  5. psql -U postgres -d themonitor -f database/db_manifest.sql > manifest_baru.txt
  6. Compare-Object (gc manifest_lama.txt) (gc manifest_baru.txt)     -> tanpa keluaran = identik
